# Saath screen recording: what to tap, and when

About 8 minutes. Follow the steps in order. Say the lines in `docs/VOICEOVER.md` while you do them, or record the screen first and add your voice after.
Buttons are written in **bold**, exactly as they appear on screen. If a name differs a little, tap the closest one.

## Before you press record
1. On the PC, in `D:\Projects\saath-rn`, run `npx expo start`.
2. Open the app in Expo Go on your phone (same Wi-Fi as the PC).
3. Turn on Do Not Disturb so no notification pops up in the video.
4. Start the phone's screen recorder.
5. For a fresh start, open **Me**, scroll to the bottom and tap **Reset app data**. Then set **Who is this for** to **Elder**. Go back to **Today**.

---

## 1. Today (about 1 minute)
1. Stay on **Today** for 3 seconds so the screen is clear (greeting, big "I'm OK" seal, tasks).
2. Tap the first task (for example **Lunch**). Watch the tick draw and a dot on the ring fill.
3. Tap one more task.
4. Tap **I'm OK**. The message "Your circle knows you are fine." appears.
5. Scroll down a little to show the rest of the list, then back up.

## 2. Language and large text (about 45 seconds)
1. Tap **Me** at the bottom.
2. Turn on **Large text**. Scroll briefly, then turn it off.
3. Under **Language**, choose **हिन्दी**. Tap **आज** (Today) to show Hindi, then go back to **मैं** (Me) and switch to **English** again.
4. Still on **Me**, point out **Who is this for** (Teen, Elder, Adult) and **Where are you now** (New diagnosis, Stable, Hard time).
5. Show **Keep watching in the background**. Turn it on and let the note appear that it needs the installed app (this is expected in Expo Go). Turn it off.

## 3. Circle and privacy (about 1 minute 15 seconds)
1. Tap **Circle**.
2. Show the member list (Asha, a neighbour, an ASHA worker, Dr. Rao).
3. On one member, tap **Routine**, then **Safety only**, then **Full** to show the privacy dial moves.
4. Tap the **Time off** switch for one member, then switch it off.
5. Point to the phone number lines: a hidden number reads "Number hidden until they agree".
6. Scroll to **Invite someone**. Type a name, enter a short number like `123`, tap **Send invite link**. The error "Enter a 10-digit mobile number." appears.
7. Now type a valid 10-digit number (for example `9876543210`) and tap **Send invite link**. Then tap **Mark joined (demo)** if it is shown.
8. Scroll to **Ask for help** and tap it, then choose **For 1 hour**.
9. Scroll to the bottom to show **Who viewed what**.

## 4. Safety and the fall alert (about 1 minute 30 seconds)
1. Tap **Safety**.
2. Tap **Simulate a fall**. The full-screen red alert with the countdown opens.
3. Let the countdown run for about 5 seconds. Show **Confidence** and the **Reaching** line with **Call** and **Send message**.
4. Tap **I'm OK**.
5. Tap **Simulate a fall** again. This time tap **False alarm**.
6. Scroll down to **How Saath learned from you** to show the baseline moved.
7. Tap **Simulate long quiet** and show the alert says it was quiet for a long time. Tap **I'm OK**.
8. Show the tally (**Alerts**, **Escalated**, **False alarm**).
9. Optional: let one alert run to the end (30 seconds) to show escalation to the next person, then tap **I'm OK**.

## 5. Care (about 1 minute 15 seconds)
1. Tap **Care**.
2. Tap **Add sugar reading**, enter `65` (a low value), and save. The low-sugar steps appear (sugar, recheck in 15 minutes, call family, a clinic or an ASHA).
3. Add another reading, for example `180`, to show the chart and the advice change.
4. Tap **Insulin taken** and **Ate a meal**. Point out the line "Saath records only the time, never the amount."
5. Scroll to **Medicine stock**. Show tablets left, days left and the status (Stock is fine / Refill soon / Refill now).
6. Scroll to **Care budget**. Tap **Add cost**, enter an amount, choose a category such as **Medicine**, and save. Show **This month** and **Last month**.

## 6. Plan (about 2 minutes)
1. Tap **Plan**. The seven tiles appear.
2. **Handing over care:** open it, show the four steps (Parent does, Together, You with backup, Independent). Tap **I am ready for the next step**, then say a parent must agree. Tap **Back**.
3. **Visit prep:** open it, show the facts, tap **Add question** and type a short question. Tap **Back**.
4. **Health passport:** open it, tick a few items under **Include in the passport**, tap **Show QR code**. Hold on the QR for 3 seconds. Tap **Back**.
5. **Weekly check-in:** open it, tap a face (for example **Good**). The kind message appears. Tap **Back**.
6. **Food guide:** open it, show **Your plate**, then tap **Rice** and **Dal** under **What did you eat?**. Tap **Back**.
7. **Body check:** open it, tick two or three foot items, tap **Save foot check**. Tap **Take a photo for your diary**, then **Don't allow** on the permission box (to show the app still works). Scroll to **Diabetes risk check**, enter an age, choose options, tap **See my score**. Tap **Back**.
8. **School plan:** open it, show **Card for the teacher**. Tap **Back**.

## 7. Other roles (about 1 minute)
1. At the very top, use the role strip to switch the demo view to **Family**. Go to **Today** and show **All is well**.
2. Switch to **Neighbour**. Show "You get safety alerts only."
3. Switch to **ASHA** or **Clinician**. Show the households list (**Households by need**) and **One-page summary**.
4. Switch back to **Person**.

## 8. Teen mode (about 45 seconds)
1. Tap **Me**, set **Who is this for** to **Teen**.
2. The guardian consent screen opens (**Ask a parent or guardian**). Show the explanation.
3. Enter a guardian name and a 10-digit phone number, then tap **My guardian agrees**.
4. The teen home opens with the handover track. Go to **Circle** and show that a guardian keeps at least **Routine**.
5. When done, tap **Me** and set **Who is this for** back to **Elder**.

## 9. Close (about 15 seconds)
Go to **Today** and hold still for 3 seconds. Stop the recording.

---

## Quick checklist before sending
- The video shows every tab: Today, Circle, Safety, Care, Plan, Me.
- You showed the fall alert, the privacy dial, the QR code and Hindi.
- Say out loud that invitations, messages and syncing are simulated, and that the fall detector is tested only on an emulator.
